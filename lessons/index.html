Guitar lessons

In person, in Maastricht. Acoustic or electric, at your level and with the music you want to play.

Styles

Bring the songs you love; that's where we start. Most of what people want to play falls into one of these:

  • Rock and popRiffs, rhythm guitar, solos.
  • BluesShuffles, bends, improvising over twelve bars.
  • Acoustic and folkStrumming, fingerpicking, accompanying yourself or others.
  • Funk and soulTight rhythm playing and rich chord voicings, up to neo-soul.

I also play a bit of flamenco, gypsy jazz and jazz, and I'm happy to give you a taste of their sound and techniques. They are deep traditions that take a lifetime to master, though: if that's your goal, a teacher who specialises in that style is the better choice.

Lessons are on acoustic or electric guitar, whichever you play (or want to play). Curious what musicians I've played with say? See About.

Levels

  • BeginnerNever played, or just started. How the guitar works, choosing an instrument, first chords and rhythms.
  • IntermediateYou know your way around a few chords. Playing by ear, finding your way on the fretboard, the electric signal chain, recording yourself.
  • AdvancedStyle-specific technique, harmony and improvisation.

Every student is different, so the lessons follow your experience, your taste and your goals rather than a fixed method.

How I teach

Music is a language, and like any language you learn it by using it. I don't think talent matters as much as people think; time with your instrument does. I work from four connected angles:

  • Feeling. Play music you love. If you don't feel it, don't play it.
  • Hearing. Train your ears so you can play along with records and with other people, without depending on sheet music.
  • Skills. The physical side: chord shapes, picking and fingerpicking, soloing technique.
  • Theory. Why a chord progression or melody sounds the way it does. Useful for improvising and songwriting, and never a goal in itself.
