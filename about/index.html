I'm Martijn Tennekes, a guitarist living in Maastricht.

Music has been part of my life since I was a child. I picked up the guitar at nineteen and taught myself, with books, sheet music, online lessons and above all my ears. It's a road without an end, and that's exactly what I like about it.

Over the years I've played rhythm and lead guitar in all kinds of bands, from classic rock and blues rock to alternative rock, pop and gospel. Meanwhile my taste kept widening: funk, soul and jazz, songwriting and music theory, music from outside the Western tradition, and lately flamenco and gypsy jazz.

By day I have a long career in applied data science, in which teaching has always played a big part: courses and workshops for government agencies and international organisations. My background is in mathematics and computer science, and that structured way of thinking comes in handy for music theory, and for teaching in general.

What I enjoy most, in both worlds, is combining structure with creativity. In data science it drew me to data visualization rather than model fitting. On the guitar I like to explore theory and chord shapes systematically, and then put them to use to make music. 10Keyz is where it all comes together: guitar lessons, live music, and SpiceMap, an app that turns music theory into pictures.
