I'm Martijn Tennekes, a guitarist living in Maastricht.

I picked up the guitar at nineteen and taught myself, with books, online lessons and above all my ears. Since then I've played in all kinds of bands, from classic rock and blues rock to alternative rock, pop and gospel, while my taste kept widening: funk, soul, jazz, and lately flamenco and gypsy jazz. It's a road without an end, and that's exactly what I like about it.

Teaching has been part of my work for years: in my day job in data science, I teach courses to professionals. What I enjoy most is combining structure with creativity. On the guitar, that means exploring theory and chord shapes systematically, and then using them to make music. SpiceMap, my app that turns music theory into pictures, comes from that same mix.
