SpiceMap

SpiceMap shows scales and chords as a map, with every interval in its own colour, so you can see how they are built and how they relate. It's the same palette as the colour strip at the top of this site.

The colours

Every colour stands for an interval: the distance in semitones from the root, the home note of a scale or chord. The colour strip at the top of every page on this site is the full set, from root to major seventh.

  • 1 Root
  • ♭2 Minor second
  • 2 Major second
  • ♭3 Minor third
  • 3 Major third
  • 4 Perfect fourth
  • ♭5 Tritone
  • 5 Perfect fifth
  • ♭6 Minor sixth
  • 6 Major sixth
  • ♭7 Minor seventh
  • 7 Major seventh

The root is white and the fifth is grey. Those are the two most stable notes, present in almost every scale and chord, so they stay neutral. That leaves the colours for the notes that give a scale or chord its character.
