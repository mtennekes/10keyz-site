SpiceMap

SpiceMap shows scales and chords on a guitar neck or a piano keyboard. With the abacus you can build your own scales and play them.

Every scale and chord has a root note. The other notes each sit at a fixed distance from it, called an interval, and every interval has its own sound. In SpiceMap every interval also has its own colour, so you can see at a glance where the different flavours are on the neck.
