SpiceMap support

Something not working, or a question about SpiceMap? Email support@10keyz.com and I'll get back to you.

It helps if you mention your device, operating system and (for the web version) browser.

FAQ

Where can I use SpiceMap?
In any modern browser at spicemap.10keyz.com. An iOS app is coming; Android is planned.
Do I need an account?
No. SpiceMap has no accounts and collects no personal data.
Where are my settings stored?
Only on your device. If you clear your browser's site data or delete the app, your settings are reset.
How do I unlock the full version?
Buy it through the checkout in the app. Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, which emails you a license key. Enter that key in SpiceMap to unlock it.
I lost my license key, or my unlock is gone.
Your key is in the email from Lemon Squeezy. You can enter it again on any device. If you can't find it, email me with the address you used for the purchase.
